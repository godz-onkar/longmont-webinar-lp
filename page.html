Questions before you sign up

Is it a sales pitch?

Dr. Jani will teach, take questions, and explain how to request a consultation if you want one. The webinar is free, and registering doesn’t commit you to anything.

I’ve already had PRP somewhere else. Is this still for me?

Yes. Part of the webinar covers why PRP works for some people and not others. You’ll come away knowing what to ask about the shot you had.

What’s the difference between PRP and a bone marrow injection?

PRP uses platelets concentrated from a small sample of your own blood. A bone marrow injection uses material drawn from your own bone marrow, which contains a different mix of cells and growth factors. Dr. Jani covers when each one is worth discussing.

Does insurance cover PRP or bone marrow injections?

Usually not. They’re generally considered elective, so most people pay for them directly.
