Section by section

  1. Sticky bar. Desktop: navy bar at the top with the date and the button; it slides in once the hero button scrolls away. Mobile: button at the bottom. Both hide whenever another button, a form or the closing section is on screen, so two gold buttons never show at once.
  2. Hero. Navy band. Slim logo line, eyebrow, then the headline in two sizes: the first two sentences as a smaller dimmed lead-in, "There’s a step in between." as the big line. Subhead. Button with the countdown pill and event line beside it, "Four quick fields" under it. Desktop: Dr. Jani’s photo on the right with his name on the photo. Mobile: no big photo; a round headshot with his name sits above the date, and the button lands on the first screen.
  3. "This is for you if" card. White card overlapping the bottom edge of the hero, four lines: the joints, told surgery is coming, already had PRP, bringing a spouse or family member. Desktop: 2 by 2. Mobile: one column, right under the hero.
  4. Credibility bar. Five items in one strip with hairline dividers: 5.0 on Google · 15 reviews, board-certified, Penn residency, Taos fellowship, 10+ years in Longmont. Two columns on mobile.
  5. The problem. The page’s quiet section: three short paragraphs and the "step in between" diagram, the page’s motif (three stops on one line, the middle stop drawn as the gold sun from the Longmont logo). Desktop: diagram on the right, horizontal. Mobile: the diagram turns vertical so the labels stay readable.
  6. What you’ll leave knowing. Heading and button on the left (they stay in view while scrolling on desktop), five numbered rows on the right with hairline dividers. On mobile the button is hidden here and the bottom bar carries it.
  7. Meet your presenter. Light blue band, square photo left with the footbag line as a gold-ruled caption under it, three paragraphs right.
  8. What patients say. Its own band in a slightly deeper blue (#DCE7F3) with a hairline on top, so it reads as a new section after the presenter. Three Google reviews with five stars each, a "Read all 15 reviews on Google" link (opens Google Maps in a new tab), the results line, and a button.
  9. Who this webinar isn’t for. One short block: heading left, three "People who…" lines right with small gray dashes; stacked on mobile. The page’s one honest-limits block. (The "good fit" column was cut; the hero card covers who it’s for.)
  10. How the webinar works. Four numbered steps on one line (the path line again, without the sun). Two columns on tablet, stacked on mobile.
  11. Form. White card with a soft shadow. The top row repeats the round headshot and the event line, so the date is in view at the moment of signing up. Labels above the fields, gold button full width, replay line under it, then the text-message consent line in small print with the Privacy Policy link.
  12. FAQ. Five questions, closed by default, chevron opens each.
  13. Final CTA and footer. Navy band, a small three-dot version of the path, headline, closing line, event line, button. Footer inside the same band: logo, address and phone as on Google, email, disclaimer.

Version B (A/B test)

  • Same page, same copy. The hero’s right column holds the registration form instead of the photo, and the hero button is removed (the form is the action). The round headshot sits under the subhead on desktop and mobile.
  • On mobile the form comes right after the date line, before the "This is for you if" card.
  • The form section further down stays, so step 1 of "How the webinar works" ("Fill out the form below") is still true there.
  • Build as a separate GHL page and split the ad traffic 50/50. Same form, same tags, so registrations compare cleanly.

How the page behaves

  • The countdown pill counts days to November 17 in Mountain time: "4 days left," then "Tomorrow at 7 PM MT," then "Today at 7 PM MT," then it hides.
  • Every "Save my free spot" button scrolls to the form. Off-page links: the Google reviews link (new tab), the Privacy Policy (new tab, under the form and in the footer), and the phone and email in the footer.
  • No scroll animations. Buttons darken slightly on hover. The sticky bar slide is turned off for people who reduce motion.

Checked on this build

  • Desktop 1440 and mobile 390 reviewed screen by screen, versions A and B. No sideways scroll on mobile. Zero console errors, zero em dashes, no dead links, no broken images.
  • Version A: the hero button sits at 533px on a 900px desktop screen and 554px on an 844px phone. The mobile hero ends at 673px.
  • Text contrast passes AA everywhere (body text 17.8:1, secondary 6.2:1 or better, button text 11.4:1).
