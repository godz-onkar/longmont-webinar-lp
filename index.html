Section by section

  1. Sticky bar. Desktop: navy bar at the top with the date and the button; it slides in once the hero button scrolls away. Mobile: button at the bottom from the first screen. Both hide while the hero button, the form or the closing section is on screen.
  2. Hero. Navy band. Logo, eyebrow, three-sentence headline (first two sentences dimmed, the answer bright), subhead, the five "This is for you if" lines, countdown pill and event line, one button. Dr. Jani’s photo on the right on desktop, below the button on mobile. On a 390 phone the first screen shows the eyebrow, headline, subhead and the first two lines; the bottom bar carries the button.
  3. Credibility bar. Five items in one strip with hairline dividers: 5.0 on Google, board-certified, Penn residency, Taos fellowship, 10+ years in Longmont. Two columns on mobile.
  4. The problem. The page’s quiet section: three short paragraphs on the left, the "step in between" diagram on the right. The diagram is the page’s motif: three stops on one line, with the middle stop drawn as the gold sun from the Longmont logo.
  5. What you’ll leave knowing. Heading and button on the left (they stay in view while scrolling on desktop), five numbered rows on the right with hairline dividers.
  6. Meet your presenter. Light blue band, photo left, three paragraphs right.
  7. What patients say. Same band. Three Google reviews as quotes, the results line, and a button.
  8. Who this webinar fits. Two columns, "good fit" with a navy rule, "probably not" with a gray one. Stacked on mobile.
  9. How the webinar works. Four numbered steps on one line (the path line again, without the sun). Two columns on tablet, stacked on mobile.
  10. Form. White card with a soft shadow, labels above the fields, gold button full width, replay line under it.
  11. FAQ. Five questions, closed by default, chevron opens each.
  12. Final CTA and footer. Navy band, a small three-dot version of the path, headline, closing line, event line, button. Footer inside the same band: logo, address and phone as on Google, email, disclaimer.

How the page behaves

  • The countdown pill counts days to November 17 in Mountain time: "4 days left," then "Tomorrow at 7 PM MT," then "Today at 7 PM MT," then it hides.
  • Every "Save my free spot" button scrolls to the form. Nothing links off the page except the phone and email in the footer.
  • No scroll animations. Buttons darken slightly on hover. The sticky bar slide is turned off for people who reduce motion.

Checked on this build

  • Desktop 1440 and mobile 390 reviewed screen by screen. No sideways scroll on mobile. Zero console errors, zero em dashes, no dead links.
  • Text contrast passes AA everywhere (body text 17.8:1, secondary 6.2:1 or better, button text 11.4:1).
